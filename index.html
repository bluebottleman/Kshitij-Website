What’s work like

I don’t believe in loving my first idea. I believe in a process that keeps me honest — one loop, six steps, repeated until the work is actually right.

Every project I take on moves through the same loop: talk to real users, place a clear bet, prototype fast, ship the smallest useful version, watch what actually happens, then decide — kill it, keep it, or double down. Then I go around again.
